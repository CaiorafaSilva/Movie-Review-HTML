Movie Review: No Country for old man

In 1980 West Texas, a welder named Llewelyn Moss stumbles upon the aftermath of a drug deal gone wrong and takes a case containing two million dollars. As he runs, he is hunted by Anton Chigurh, a relentless hitman, while aging Sheriff Ed Tom Bell tries to catch up with them both. A tense and bleak thriller about fate, violence, and a world that is changing faster than the people trying to understand it.
